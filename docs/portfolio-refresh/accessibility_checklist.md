# Accessibility checklist

- Use semantic `section` and `article` structure with headings in order.
- Provide descriptive link labels such as `Open RustFlow repository`; decorative icons are hidden from assistive technology where applicable.
- Keep outbound links identifiable through their action labels and keyboard reachable.
- Do not render an interactive control for EvoLife until a public destination exists.
- Preserve contrast between the dark background, light body text, and gold/cyan action elements.
- Keep the abstract home-card image alt text descriptive of its decorative role rather than claiming a product screenshot.
